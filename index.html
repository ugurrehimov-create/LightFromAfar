Light from Afar
A home for people who love space

Look up on a clear night and the sky can feel like a question you have always been almost able to answer. Light from Afar is a place to follow that feeling — to explore the real sky, understand distant targets, plan how they fit your equipment, and remember what happened when you finally captured them.

It is built around the work astrophotographers actually do: discover, evaluate, frame, plan, connect, observe, capture, record, review, and eventually share. The goal is not a leaderboard or a collection of invented activity. Your observing record stays useful even when the community layer is not connected.

It was made for the feeling of being small and grateful under a dark sky — and for anyone who wants better tools while they are there.
